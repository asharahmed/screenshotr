Not just screenshots. Anything.

Every way into Screenshotr ends the same way: one upload, one link on your clipboard, and an entry in your history.

  • Capture ⇧⌘4 for an area or a window, ⇧⌘3 for the whole screen. The shortcuts you already know.
  • Finder Right-click any file and choose Services › Upload with Screenshotr, or Open With.
  • Clipboard Copy an image or some files, then upload them with one shortcut of your choosing.
  • Drop Drag files onto any Screenshotr window, or pick them from the menu bar.

Your storage, not ours.

Screenshotr has no server. Point it at what you already use, save as many destinations as you like, and switch with one click. Cloudflare R2 needs a single token; ShareX uploaders import from a file.

    Private by design.

    • Nothing in betweenFiles go straight from your Mac to your destination. There's no Screenshotr account, server or analytics.
    • Keys in the KeychainCredentials live in your login Keychain, never in a plain-text settings file.
    • Only when you askScreenshotr looks at your screen when you take a shot, and at nothing else.
